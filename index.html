Canada Customs – Your Trusted Partner in Brokerage and Compliance Solutions

With a strong foundation built on decades of experience and a reputation for reliability, JMD Customs Brokers Inc. has become a recognized leader in customs brokerage and compliance services across Canada and beyond. Our team of certified professionals ensures every shipment is cleared efficiently, accurately, and in full compliance with all regulations.

Whether your goods are entering Canada from global markets or moving regionally, JMD Customs Brokers Inc. offers tailored solutions to navigate complex customs processes and minimize delays.

Our advanced technology platform, combined with our in-depth regulatory knowledge and direct connection to the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA), ensures that your shipments are processed quickly and without complications.

From small parcels to full container loads, and from first-time importers to enterprise-level supply chains, JMD Customs Brokers Inc. consistently delivers high-performance customs brokerage services with a focus on speed, transparency, and compliance.

We take pride in being a trusted partner for Canadian importers, offering dependable support and proactive service that simplifies the customs clearance journey.

JMD Customs Brokers Inc.’ Canada Customs services include:

  • Full electronic customs entries with compliance monitoring
  • Duty deferral, duty minimization, and tax advisory
  • Direct integration with your ERP or shipping systems
  • End-to-end support across industries and import volumes
